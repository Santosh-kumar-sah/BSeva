Bihar Sahayak 🇮🇳
AI-Powered Government Scheme & Career Intelligence Platform for Bihar
Discover. Understand. Decide. Connect.
Bihar Sahayak is a Bihar-focused GovTech platform designed to help citizens, students, job seekers, farmers, and other eligible beneficiaries discover relevant government schemes, understand eligibility requirements, identify required documents, explore career and skill-development opportunities, and navigate to the appropriate official government service.
The platform is designed as an intelligence and discovery layer over existing government services, not as a replacement for official Bihar Government portals.

🚀 Project Overview
Bihar has numerous government schemes, scholarships, employment programs, skill-development initiatives, agricultural subsidies, welfare programs, and citizen services.
However, information is distributed across multiple departments and portals.
A citizen may need to understand:
Which schemes exist?
Whether they are eligible?
Why they are eligible?
Which documents are required?
Which department manages the scheme?
Where should they apply?
What is the next step?
What career or skill opportunities are available to them?
Bihar Sahayak attempts to simplify this discovery process.
❗ Problem Statement
Current Problem
Government services are increasingly becoming digital, but digitization does not automatically mean discoverability.
A citizen may know that government schemes exist but may not know:
Which scheme? ↓ Am I eligible? ↓ Why am I eligible? ↓ What documents do I need? ↓ Where do I apply? ↓ What happens after applying?
The information may be available, but it is often distributed across departments, portals, PDFs, notifications, and websites.
💡 Proposed Solution
Bihar Sahayak creates a unified discovery and intelligence layer.
Citizen ↓ Basic Profile ↓ Eligibility Analysis ↓ Relevant Schemes ↓ Benefits ↓ Required Documents ↓ Application Instructions ↓ Official Government Portal
For career guidance:
Education ↓ Skills ↓ Interests ↓ Career Options ↓ Required Skills ↓ Training Opportunities ↓ Employment Opportunities
🎯 Vision
Make Bihar's government opportunities easier to discover, understand, and access.
🎯 Mission
Bihar Sahayak aims to:
Improve awareness of government schemes.
Reduce confusion around eligibility.
Simplify government information.
Provide Hindi-first access.
Connect citizens to official services.
Provide personalized career and skill guidance.
Help government understand citizen demand through aggregated analytics.
Reduce information asymmetry between citizens and government programs.
🧠 Core Product Principle
Bihar Sahayak is NOT:
a replacement for government portals

a replacement for government departments
Bihar Sahayak IS:

⚙️ Eligibility Rule Engine
Eligibility should NOT depend entirely on AI.
Use deterministic rules wherever possible.
Example:
if ( user.isBiharResident === true && user.age >= 18 && user.familyIncome <= 300000 ) { return "potentially_eligible"; }
🧠 Rule Types
Supported rule types:
AGE_MIN AGE_MAX INCOME_MAX INCOME_MIN GENDER CATEGORY RESIDENCE EDUCATION OCCUPATION LAND_SIZE EMPLOYMENT_STATUS SKILL DISTRICT BLOCK DOCUMENT
🔀 Rule Operators
Support:
AND OR NOT GREATER_THAN LESS_THAN EQUAL IN BETWEEN
Example:
{ "operator": "AND", "conditions": [ { "field": "residence", "operator": "EQUAL", "value": "Bihar" }, { "field": "income", "operator": "LESS_THAN_OR_EQUAL", "value": 300000 } ] }
🟡 Eligibility Status
Never simply return:
Eligible
Instead use:
Potentially Eligible Likely Not Eligible Needs Verification
Because final eligibility belongs to the relevant authority.
